George

George is a little over two years old and has built his life around three things: family, tennis balls, and the lake. Most mornings begin with snuggling the kids, while summer turns him into “Captain Corgi,” happily taking his place on the water.

His greatest obsession, however, is fetch. Give George a tennis ball and he is ready to work. There does not appear to be a reasonable stopping point.

What makes George special is how naturally friendly he is. He loves his family, wants to be included in whatever is happening, and approaches just about everyone as though they are already a friend. George embraces the quote "there is no such thing as strangers, just friends you haven't met yet."

Scouting Report

Elite snuggler. Strong lake presence. Relentless pursuit of the tennis ball.

Beans

Beans has lived a surprisingly eventful life. She was a teen mom, has a habit of grunting when startled, and has been informed that, for her height, she technically falls into the clinically obese category. Her family respectfully disputes the scouting report.

She is not a princess that requires expensive or high dollar toys. She treasures the red straws from Chick-Fil-A. Place one red straw on the floor and suddenly she has purpose, direction and something worth getting out of bed for.

Underneath the quirks, Beans is an exceptionally gentle cat. After spending years as the only cat in the house, she welcomed a frightened and neglected young cat into the family. Beans quickly accepted her as a sister and helped her become comfortable, confident, and at home. They have recently launched a tiny nonprofit for emotionally overwhelmed cats.

Scouting Report

Calm under pressure. Low center of gravity. Motivated almost exclusively by red straws.
