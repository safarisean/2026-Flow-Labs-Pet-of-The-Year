Beans

Owner: Sam Blackwell
The Basics

Former teen mom. Surprise grunter. Technically obese for her height, according to people who clearly do not understand her build.

What You Need to Know

Beans spent years as a solo cat before welcoming a scared, neglected young kitty into the family and helping her come out of her shell.

Greatest Love

Not toys. Not catnip. Red Chick-fil-A straws.

Scouting Report

Low center of gravity. Excellent composure. Unusual straw-based motivation.

The Case for Beans

Beans is calm, well-behaved, and the kind of cat who quietly makes the whole house better. She accepted a frightened new cat as her sister and helped her become comfortable and confident.
